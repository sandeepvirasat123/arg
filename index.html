The ARG Verified check

We do the homework before you visit.

A beautiful sample flat tells you nothing about the paperwork behind it. Here's what our team reviews before a property earns the ARG Verified tag.

  • Ownership & title

    Sale deed and chain of ownership matched with the seller.

  • RERA & approvals

    Project registration, sanctioned plan and OC/CC status.

  • Dues & encumbrances

    Pending maintenance, loans or liens flagged early.

  • Physical inspection

    Walls, fittings, seepage and fixtures looked at in person.

  • Price benchmark

    Asking rate compared with recent nearby transactions.

  • Neighbourhood check

    Connectivity, schools, hospitals and upcoming infrastructure.

Ask us anything
What does “ARG Verified” mean?

It means our team has reviewed the property's ownership documents, RERA registration and approvals, checked for pending dues, inspected the unit in person and compared its price with recent nearby deals. We share what we find — including any concerns — before you decide.

Do you charge buyers a fee?

Our fee structure is shared upfront in writing before any work begins, so there are no surprises later. Call us and we'll explain it for your specific requirement.

Which areas do you cover?

We focus on Greater Noida West, Noida, Greater Noida and Ghaziabad, including Gaur City, Bisrakh, Techzone 4, Indirapuram, Raj Nagar Extension and the Yamuna Expressway belt.

Can you help with resale and new projects?

Yes. We work with resale homes, ready-to-move units and selected under-construction projects, as well as commercial spaces and plots.

I'm a broker or developer. Can I work with ARG?

Yes. We partner with brokers, channel partners, developers and referral partners. Visit our Partner page or call us to get started.

How quickly can I visit a property?

In most cases we can arrange a site visit within 24 hours, seven days a week, at a time that suits you.
