Selling FAQs

Before you sell

Practical answers for property owners.

Ask us anything
Is the valuation really free?

Yes. There's no charge and no obligation to list with us. You'll get a price range and the comparable deals behind it.

How long does it take to sell?

It depends on pricing, location and the property's condition. A well-priced home in an active society typically attracts serious interest within weeks. We'll give you an honest estimate after the valuation.

Do I need to renovate before selling?

Usually not. We may suggest small, low-cost fixes — fresh paint, repairs to visible damage — that make a real difference to buyers. We'll never push expensive renovations.

Can I sell a property that still has a home loan?

Yes. We coordinate with your bank to close the loan from the sale proceeds and release the original documents at registration.

What documents will I need?

Typically your sale deed or allotment letter, previous chain of documents, ID and address proof, PAN, society NOC, latest maintenance and tax receipts, and loan closure details if applicable. We'll give you a personalised checklist.
