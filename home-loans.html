Typical loan documents

Salaried applicants

  • PAN and Aadhaar

  • 6 months' bank statements

Self-employed applicants

Loan FAQs

Home loan basics

Common questions from first-time borrowers.

Ask us anything
How much home loan can I get?

Lenders usually look at your income, existing EMIs, age and credit score. As a rough guide, total EMIs are often kept within about half of your monthly income. We'll give you a personalised estimate.

Do you charge for loan assistance?

Loan assistance is part of our service for clients buying through ARG. We'll confirm everything in writing upfront.

Fixed or floating rate — which is better?

Floating rates move with the market and are the most common choice in India; fixed rates offer certainty but are usually higher. We'll explain the trade-offs for your situation.

Can I get a loan for a resale property?

Yes. Lenders finance resale homes after checking the property's documents and value. Our upfront verification helps that review go faster.

Does a co-applicant help?

Adding an earning co-applicant, such as a spouse, can increase eligibility and may offer benefits. We'll help you decide whether it makes sense.
