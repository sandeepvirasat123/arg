Partner FAQs

Questions before you apply

Have a question that is not here? Our team is happy to talk it through.

Speak with Our Team
What is an ARG Channel Partner?

An ARG Channel Partner (CP) is an individual or firm who works with Asset Realty Group by introducing property opportunities, buyer requirements, or both. There is one programme and one CP identity.

Do I have to choose between property and buyer connections?

No. They are two activities inside one partnership, not separate partner types. The form asks what interests you so we can have a useful first conversation. Permissions for each activity are confirmed during onboarding, according to ARG policy.

Who can apply?

Real-estate brokers, independent property consultants, local agents, society-level contacts, referral professionals and anyone with genuine property-owner or buyer relationships. You can apply as an individual or as a firm.

How much commission will I earn?

We do not publish a standard rate or promise any level of income. Eligibility, attribution, commission or split, milestones and payment conditions are set out in written partner terms for each relevant transaction, subject to ARG approval.

How is an opportunity attributed to me?

Source attribution is documented in your written terms. Where one CP works on both property and buyer activities, the records for each are kept separately where policy requires, so a contribution on one side is not mixed up with the other.

What happens after I submit the form?

The ARG team reviews your enquiry and contacts you for an intro call and fit check. If it is a good fit, onboarding and written terms follow. Submitting an enquiry does not activate portal access or create an agreement.

Is there anything to pay to submit an enquiry?

No. Submitting this enquiry is free. Any further programme details are explained during onboarding, in writing, before you decide to proceed.

Do I need to share Aadhaar, PAN or bank details now?

No. Please do not send them through this website. If any documents are needed later, the ARG team will explain what is needed and why during onboarding.

I work outside the areas listed. Can I still apply?

Yes. Mention your city or area in the form. We will tell you honestly whether there is a fit, based on the markets we currently serve.

I am a developer. Is this programme for me?

This programme is for people who introduce property owners and buyers. For project or developer conversations, please speak with our team through the Contact page.
