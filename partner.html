Partner FAQs

Partnering questions

What brokers, developers and referrers usually ask us.

Ask us anything
Is there a fee to become a partner?

No. Joining is free. Partnership terms, including payouts, are agreed in writing before any deal begins.

How are payouts decided?

It depends on the type of partnership and the deal. We agree the split upfront in writing and pay as per those terms once the deal closes.

Can I partner if I work outside Greater Noida West?

Yes. Our main markets are Greater Noida West, Noida, Greater Noida and Ghaziabad, and we're happy to work with partners who bring clients from anywhere.

Will you contact my clients directly?

Only with your agreement. We respect partner relationships and keep you informed at every step of the deal.

Can developers list new projects with ARG?

Yes. We review project approvals and RERA registration first, then discuss how we can bring the right buyers to it.
