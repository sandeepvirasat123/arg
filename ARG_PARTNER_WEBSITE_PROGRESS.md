- **All pages:** self-hosted logo at `assets/img/arg-logo.jpeg`; unified CP wording on Home, About, Contact and in footers.
- **Added:** `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `.gitignore`, `api/config.example.php`. `.htaccess` now blocks config and internal notes.
