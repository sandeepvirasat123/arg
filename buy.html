Buying FAQs

Before you buy

Straight answers to the questions buyers ask us most.

Ask us anything
Is a resale property a good choice compared to a new launch?

Resale homes are usually ready to move into, so you can see exactly what you're paying for and avoid construction delays. New launches may offer payment plans and newer amenities. We'll lay out both options for your budget so you can compare fairly.

What documents should I check before buying?

At minimum: the sale deed or allotment letter, previous chain of ownership, RERA registration (for projects), occupancy or completion certificate, society NOC, and proof that maintenance and property taxes are paid. We review these for every ARG Verified property.

How much should I budget beyond the property price?

Plan for stamp duty and registration charges, legal and documentation costs, society transfer fees, and furnishing. We'll give you an itemised estimate for the specific property before you commit.

Can you help NRIs buy remotely?

Yes. We can share detailed video walkthroughs, coordinate document verification and work with your power of attorney holder for registration.

What if the property I want isn't listed here?

Share your requirement — many good homes are sold through our network before they're ever advertised. We'll search on your behalf.
